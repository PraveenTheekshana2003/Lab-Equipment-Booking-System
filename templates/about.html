About LabBook

Lab Equipment Booking System – Mini Project 2026

Overview

The Faculty maintains a shared collection of equipment used by students for coursework and projects. This web system replaces manual paper-based bookings and prevents double bookings, lost records and availability confusion.

Who can use it?

  • Students – Register, browse equipment, request bookings and track status.
  • Lab Staff – Manage equipment inventory, review requests and approve or reject them.

Equipment examples

  • Raspberry Pi kits
  • Arduino kits
  • Oscilloscopes
  • VR headsets
  • 3D printing equipment

Key business rule

An equipment item cannot have overlapping Pending or Approved bookings. A booking that starts exactly when another ends is allowed.

Contact

For questions about the system or equipment availability, please contact the Lab Staff or your course coordinator.

Built with Flask, SQLite, Flask-SQLAlchemy and Flask-Login.
